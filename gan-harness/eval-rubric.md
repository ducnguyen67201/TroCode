# Evaluation rubric

Pass threshold: 7.5 / 10.

### Design Quality (weight: 0.35)

The pacing choice is immediately understandable, visually calm, and properly integrated into the companion callout.

### Originality (weight: 0.30)

The solution feels native to a cursor-led teaching assistant rather than copying a generic media player or settings panel.

### Craft (weight: 0.25)

Labels, states, timing, keyboard behavior, accessibility, and edge cases are specific and coherent.

### Functionality (weight: 0.10)

The proposal supports manual progression, autoplay, pause/stop, task-dependent defaults, and remembered preferences without blocking the teaching flow.
